# 🧠 CleverCortex – AI-Powered Study Companion

CleverCortex is an intelligent study planning and learning assistant designed to help students organize their studies, track progress, and improve learning efficiency using Artificial Intelligence. The application generates personalized study schedules based on syllabus, deadlines, and available study time.

---

## 🌟 Features

### 📚 Smart Study Planning
- Upload syllabus and study materials
- Generate personalized study schedules
- Goal-based learning plans
- Deadline-aware planning

### 🤖 AI Learning Assistant
- AI-powered study recommendations
- Topic prioritization
- Personalized learning guidance
- Adaptive study strategies

### ⏰ Task Management
- Daily study goals
- Task scheduling
- Progress tracking
- Study reminders

### 📊 Performance Analytics
- Learning progress visualization
- Completion statistics
- Subject-wise performance tracking
- Productivity insights

### 📝 Notes Management
- Store study notes
- Organize subject materials
- Quick revision support
- Resource management

### 🎯 Exam Preparation
- Revision planning
- Exam countdown
- Important topic identification
- Last-minute preparation support
